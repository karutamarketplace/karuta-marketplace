Bot Commands

List of commands available in Saia Market.

/Market
Access all features of Saia Market with buttons.
The only non-Karuta command you need to remember.
k!cardinfo (Karuta Command!)
List your card, or bid on other listed cards.
k!inventory (Karuta Command!)
List your inventory items. k!bits to list your bits.
k!userinfo (Karuta Command!)
View profiles, rate and report users.
/Market, /Browse, /My_Listings, /Bulk_List

How to Get Started

Add Saia Market to your server in three simple steps.

1

Invite Bot

Click the invite button to authorize Saia Market on your Discord server.

2

Set Permissions

Ensure that Saia Market is given the ticked permissions.

3

Start Trading

Type /Market in any channel to get started.
