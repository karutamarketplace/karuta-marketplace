Bot Commands

List of commands available in Saia Market.

s!market
Open or browse the global Karuta marketplace.
s!sell <card/item>
List a specific Karuta card or item for sale/trade.
s!search <query>
Search active market listings by keyword or card code.
s!trade <user>
Initiate a trade offer with another Discord member.
s!help
Display full list of commands and usage instructions.
